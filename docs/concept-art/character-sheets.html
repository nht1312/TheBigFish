Costume and look

  • Áo bà ba in faded mauve: loose, buttoned down the front, side slits at the hip, long sleeves. It's a house shirt, worn soft, not a festival áo dài.
  • Quần lãnh đen: wide black silk pants to the ankle, with a slight sheen.
  • Plastic house sandals (dép nhựa). Hair pulled back in a low bun, a few loose strands.
  • Hands that work every day: short nails, no rings except maybe a thin gold band.
  • Usually seated on a low plastic stool, sorting greens (lặt rau) from a basket. In the ending scene she stands in the doorway.

Costume and look

  • Nón lá: a palm-leaf conical hat, about 60 cm across the brim, sun-bleached, with a thin chin strap.
  • Faded khaki work shirt with a breast pocket, sleeves rolled to just below the elbow, collar open.
  • Dark grey trousers rolled once at the ankle. Black rubber sandals.
  • Skin darkened by decades outdoors. Short grey hair and grey brows, with deep lines at the eyes.
  • Sits on a blue plastic stool at the canal edge with a white bucket beside him. His hands stay on the rod, even when he talks.

Modelling

  • Realistic adult proportions. Heads about 1/7 of height, not chibi.
  • 8k–15k triangles per character, including clothing and hat.
  • PBR textures at 2048 px: albedo, normal, roughness. Cloth should read as worn cotton and silk.

Rig and export

  • Humanoid skeleton that retargets in Godot 4 (Mixamo-style bone names are fine).
  • Export glTF binary (.glb) with 1 unit = 1 m, facing −Z, feet at the origin.
  • Animations embedded and named exactly as listed on each sheet.

Dropping it in

The game loads assets/characters/<npc_id>.glb automatically when the file exists and replaces the built-in placeholder figure. No code change is needed. Seated poses must line up with the stool heights given above.
